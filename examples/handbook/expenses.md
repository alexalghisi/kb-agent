# Expenses

Submit receipts in Pleo within 30 days. Anything over 500 EUR needs your manager's approval before you buy it.

## Home office

You get a one-off 600 EUR home office budget for a chair, desk or screen, and 40 EUR per month towards internet.

## Travel

Book trains when the journey is under four hours. Flights are economy; book them through Navan so they appear on the company card.
