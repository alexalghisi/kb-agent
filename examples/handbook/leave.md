# Leave and holidays

Everyone gets 25 days of paid leave per year, plus the public holidays of the country you live in.

## Booking leave

Book leave in Factorial at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days, and add it to the team calendar.

## Sick days

If you're ill, tell your manager in Slack before your first meeting. You don't need a doctor's note for the first three days.

## Parental leave

Parents get 16 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be split into up to three blocks in the first year.
